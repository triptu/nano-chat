Chat with AI that lives on your phone.

Nano Chat talks to AI models that run on the phone itself, so nothing you write leaves it. Each companion is one ongoing chat, like a contact, with its own name, colour and personality.

Companions, kept like contacts

A fresh install has three companions. New friend makes another. Each has a name, a colour, a personality written in plain words, and a model. The personality goes to the model with every reply.

Replies keep running when you leave a chat, and Stop keeps what was written so far. If the app closes before a reply arrives, the message offers Try again.

Two models, both on the phone

Gemini Nano is the default. It is built into recent Pixel and Galaxy phones, needs no download in the app and costs it no memory.

Gemma 4 E4B is a larger model you download once, 3.7 GB from Hugging Face. It replies better, follows a personality more closely, and understands photos and voice notes. It needs a phone with 12 GB of memory or more.

Each companion picks its own model. Photos and voice notes stay in the app’s private storage.

A companion can use your phone

With Handset installed, turn on Use this phone in a companion’s edit sheet. Handset shows what Nano Chat asks for, with every source off until you switch it on.

The companion can then search what you saw on screen and in notifications, catch up on messages, read a day’s timeline, open apps, set alarms and timers, switch Do not disturb, and control what’s playing. Each step shows as a line under the reply. Tap it to see what came back.

What a companion won’t do

What a companion may do on the phone is limited on purpose. Handset’s own rules apply on top.

  • It takes at most 3 phone steps per reply.
  • It can’t read the screen, tap or type. Small models drive screens poorly.
  • Once a reply has read your content from the phone, it can’t act in that reply. A message from someone else can’t make a companion open an app or set an alarm.
  • Turning off Use this phone stops a reply that is using it.

Install it

  1. On the phone, download nano-chat.apk and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or files app.
  2. The releases page lists each APK’s SHA-256 and signing certificate, so you can check what you install. A newer APK installs over the old one and keeps your chats.
  3. Open Nano Chat and say hello to Nano. To try Gemma 4 E4B, pick it for a companion and start the download.

Privacy

Chats run on the phone. The app has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. It uses the network for two things: downloading Gemma 4 E4B from Hugging Face, and talking to Handset on the phone itself.

Turning on Use this phone gives a companion whatever you allow in Handset, such as notifications or location. The model reads that text to answer you. It stays on the phone, but it is in the chat from then on.
